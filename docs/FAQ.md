# One Stop Shop mockup: quick answers

Preview: https://onestopshop-preview.anas-dev.workers.dev
This page is for whoever presents the mockup. The other page, the notes, is the one to leave with the shop.

## Show it like this
Open it on a phone. Tap a hookah, add it to the cart, type a postal code (M5V 2T6 is Toronto). The shipping price shows up, with free pickup beside it.

## The mockup

**Is this the real store?**
No. It is a sample to show the look and feel. Nothing can be bought on it yet.

**Why aren't these our products and photos?**
They are stand-ins. Your real products, prices and photos go in when we build the real store.

**Can we change things?**
Yes. Colours, wording, layout, all of it. Tell us what you like and what you don't.

**Can the public see this?**
No. It is a private link and Google can't find it.

## Shipping

**How does the shipping price work?**
The customer types their postal code. The store works out the price from how far the order is going, how heavy it is, and how big the box is.

**Where do the prices come from?**
Canada Post's own price list for small businesses.

**Is it accurate?**
We checked all 988 prices against Canada Post's list and every one matches. The product weights in the mockup are samples, so real weights come next.

**How does it stay correct?**
Canada Post changes its prices a little every week. The real store asks Canada Post for the price at checkout, so the customer pays what you are billed.

**What does it cost to ship a hookah?**
A tall hookah from Windsor: about $57 to Montreal, $60 to Halifax, $75 to Vancouver. A hose to Toronto is about $16.

**Why that much?**
Big boxes cost more than heavy ones. A slim box can cut the price roughly in half.

**Can we offer free or flat-rate shipping?**
Yes. That is your call.

**Can customers pick up in store?**
Yes, for free.

**Can we ship to the US?**
Not in the mockup. It can be added later.

## What can be sold

**Can we sell shisha tobacco online?**
Not to other provinces. Federal law doesn't allow it, so the store doesn't list it.

**Charcoal and vapes?**
Not yet. A carrier has to agree in writing to take them first.

**Hookahs, glass, bowls, hoses and parts?**
Yes, anywhere in Canada.

## Next steps

**What happens to our current website?**
It stays up until the new store is ready.

**What do you need from us?**
Your address and hours, your product list with prices, your photos, and the weight and box size for one of each kind of product.

**How much, and how long?**
We will send both in writing after this meeting.

## If he asks something else
Write it down and say we will get back to him. Don't guess on prices, tobacco rules or dates.
