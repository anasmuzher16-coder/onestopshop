Preview: https://onestopshop-preview.anas-dev.workers.dev
For whoever is presenting. Plain answers, and where we do not know yet, it says so.

1. Open the preview on a phone. Scroll the home page, tap a hookah.
2. Tap Add to cart. Open the cart, type a postal code (try M5V 2T6 for Toronto, V6B 1A1 for Vancouver).

## About the mockup

**Is this the real store?**
No. It is a working sample to show the look and how it behaves. The products, prices and photos are stand-ins. Nothing can be bought on it.

**Why are those not our products?**
We did not have the product list or photos yet. The sample has 86 made-up products in six groups (hookahs, bases, bowls, heat, hoses, parts) so the store feels full. Real products replace them.

**Whose photos are those?**
Placeholders borrowed from other shops' websites, only to show the layout. They must be replaced with the shop's own photos before the store goes public. The preview is hidden from Google for that reason.

**Can customers find this preview?**
No. It is on a private preview address, marked as a mockup, and search engines are told not to list it.

**Can we change the colours, wording, layout?**
Yes. That is what the mockup is for. Write down what he likes and what he does not.

**Does it work on phones?**
Yes, it was built for phones first and checked at five phone screen sizes. It also works on tablets and desktop.

**Is the address and phone number right?**
The phone number (519-255-2222) and hours came from the current site. The street address is a placeholder. Ask him to confirm all three.

**Where did the Arabic go / can we have Arabic?**
We left it out until someone who reads Arabic can check it. It can be added.

## The shipping calculator

**How does it work?**
The customer types a postal code. The store packs the cart into boxes, then prices each box by how far it is going and by its weight or its size, whichever costs more. It then adds this week's fuel charge and, on orders over $100, cover for glass.

**Does it use location and weight?**
Yes, both. Location comes from the postal code. Weight comes from each product, plus the weight of the box. Box size counts too, because carriers charge for big light boxes as if they were heavy.

**Where do the prices come from?**
Canada Post's published small business price guide, the same prices a shop with a free Canada Post business account pays.

**How accurate is it?**
We checked every price in our table against Canada Post's guide: 988 of 988 match. We ran 2,328 test orders through it and all pass. What is still an estimate in the mockup is the product weights and box sizes, because they are sample numbers.

**How does it stay up to date?**
Canada Post changes its fuel charge every week (41.2% this week, 39.9% from Nov 16). In the mockup that number is typed in. In the live store, the price is asked from Canada Post at checkout, using the shop's own account, so it is always the current price and matches what the shop is billed.

**What if Canada Post's system is down?**
The store falls back on the built-in price table so checkout still works.

**Sample prices?**
From Windsor, Regular Parcel, before tax:
- A tall $649 hookah: Montreal $56.68, Halifax $60.24, Vancouver $74.96.
- One hose: Toronto $15.89, Vancouver $31.36.

**Why is shipping a hookah that much?**
Tall boxes and glass. The box size drives the price more than the weight. Packing a tall hookah in a slim box bills at about half of what a wide box does, so choosing the right boxes saves real money.

**What delivery speeds does it show?**
Canada Post Regular Parcel, Expedited Parcel and Xpresspost, each with a price and an estimated number of days. The cheapest is shown first.

**What about big orders?**
It splits them into more than one box. No box goes over 30 kg, which is Canada Post's limit.

**Is breakage covered?**
On orders over $100 the price includes Canada Post cover for the declared value and a signature on delivery. The shop's own returns and breakage policy is his to decide.

**Can we offer free shipping or a flat rate?**
Yes. Free over a set amount, a flat rate, or exact cost are all possible. That is his choice.

**Can customers pick up in store?**
Yes. Free pickup at the counter is offered beside shipping.

**Can we use Purolator, UPS or FedEx instead?**
The mockup uses Canada Post because its prices are published and it reaches every address in Canada. A second carrier can be added later so the store shows whichever is cheaper.

**Can we ship to the United States?**
The mockup is Canada only. It can be looked at later; it brings customs paperwork.

**Does the price include tax?**
The mockup shows the total before tax and says tax is added at checkout.

## What can be sold online

**Can we sell shisha tobacco online?**
Not across provinces. Federal law (Tobacco and Vaping Products Act, section 9.1) bans sending tobacco to another province for sale, and also bans advertising that offer. So the store does not list it.

**Charcoal and vapes?**
Left out for now. We need a carrier to confirm in writing that it will take them before they go in the store.

**Hookahs, glass, bowls, hoses, parts?**
Yes, anywhere in Canada.

**Do we need an age check?**
Not in the mockup. We will confirm what Ontario requires for these products before launch.

## Going live

**How do customers pay?**
Checkout is not connected in the mockup. The payment provider is chosen when we build the real store.

**How do we add or change products later?**
That gets decided in the build, based on who at the shop will be doing it and how often.

**What happens to the current GoDaddy website?**
It stays up until the new store is ready. Then the shop's web address is pointed at the new store.

**What do you need from us?**
The street address and hours, the product list with prices, weights and box sizes for one of each kind of product, photos, who packs the orders, the web address, and a Canada Post business account. The full list is in the handout.

**How much, and how long?**
Do not quote a price or a date on the spot. Say we will send both in writing after this meeting.

## If he asks something not on this list
Write it down and say we will come back with an answer. Do not guess, especially on shipping prices, tobacco rules or dates.
